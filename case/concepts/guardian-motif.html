Guardian motif (concept, not built)

Idea from the first test print: with splay the keyboard resembles the guardian from the game Tunic. The middle reads as the body, the stepped columns as the bars that stand out from it. This page keeps the concept for later. The sketch uses 18° splay per half and simplified outlines.

  • Colours: sandstone for plates and middle cover , the bottom shell the same or a little darker. Dark outlines as 0.8 mm inlays along the plate edges, like the drawn outlines of the figure.
  • Three eyes: hexagons in blue , pink and green in a triangle on the middle cover. The light dot in each centre is left open, so the cover colour shows through.
  • Centre line as a dark line down to the encoder, the knob as a hexagonal cap.
  • Multi-colour printing: the cover and the plates print upside down on their visible face, so all colours sit in the first 2–3 layers: few colour changes, little waste. The eyes need four colours (sand, blue, pink, green). Dark outlines need a fifth colour or an engraved line instead.
